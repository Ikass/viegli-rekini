What can't it do yet?

Credit notes come soon after launch, then quotes and advance invoices. There's no bank sync and no setup for several shops yet.

If you need those now, this isn't the right tool yet. I'd rather you hear that before you sign up.

Can I bring my customers over from another tool?

Not yet. You add each customer and item the first time you use them, and they're saved after that.
