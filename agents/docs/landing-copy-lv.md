## Translation notes

- **Informal "tu":** it matches the founder voice and the freelancer audience. Outvoicer also uses it. If you prefer formal "jūs", the change is mechanical (verb endings and pronouns).
- **Contrast headline:** reworded rather than translated. "You probably aren't one" would need "grāmatvedis" or "grāmatvede", and the page speaks to a woman as much as a man, so the Latvian says "nevis tiem, kam rēķins vienkārši jāizraksta".
- **"Items":** translated as "preces un pakalpojumi" (goods and services) on screens and "preces" where space is short. Check this against the glossary in the i18n plan.
- **"Legal in Latvia":** "Atbilst Latvijas likumiem" is as strong a claim as the English. The same accountant check applies to both.
- **Money:** Latvian format everywhere in the pictures: decimal comma, euro sign after the number.
