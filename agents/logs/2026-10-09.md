## 2026-10-09 — Polish + secrets hardening
Goal: Fix dark-mode invoice rendering and lock down GHCR secret handling.
What happened: Forced `.invoice-doc` to render as white paper (print palette) regardless of theme, and to fill its card edge-to-edge; moved the GHCR token to a per-machine file; ran gitleaks across full history (clean); documented per-machine secret recreation.
Decision: Store the GHCR PAT at `~/.config/viegli-rekini/ghcr-token` (out of repo) read by the committed `.kamal/secrets` loader — portable across machines. Trade-off: no env-var override (dotenv can't do `${VAR:-default}`).
Lesson: Kamal parses `.kamal/secrets` with dotenv, not bash — `${VAR:-default}` is unsupported and silently resolves wrong (`docker login` → "denied"); only `$(command)` and `$VAR` are safe. Also: invoice-doc's print palette renders near-black ink, invisible on a dark-mode card unless the doc is forced white.
Next: `fix/invoice-numbering` then `fix/money-rounding`; README rewrite before repo goes public.
